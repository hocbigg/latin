Great Courses

This collection features standout university lecture series, open courseware, and specialized digital audiovisual courses in Latin language, Roman literature, rhetoric, and manuscript culture. These resources build deep intuition for textual criticism, active Latinity, and the material and historical worlds in which Latin was composed and transmitted.

  • Active Latin, Pedagogy, and Spoken Latinity
  • Roman Literature, Epic, and Poetic Tradition
  • Roman Political Rhetoric, Law, and Material Culture
  • Late Antiquity, Medieval Latinity, and Manuscript Culture
