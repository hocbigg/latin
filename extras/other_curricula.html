Other curricula

This curriculum is one of a few efforts to inform learners how they can pursue Latin independently. Below are a few other such efforts.

  • Textkit Greek and Latin Forums Resources (community-driven guides and public domain texts)
  • Ranieri-Roberts Approach to Ancient Greek (Luke Ranieri Spreadsheet) (structured self-study sequence with readers)
  • Paideia Institute Online Programs (synchronous courses, some free materials)
  • Dickinson College Commentaries Self-Study Guides (annotated texts for independent reading)
